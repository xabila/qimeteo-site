Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. On-Device Processing on Your iPhone

QiMétéo, published by Xavier Larrodé, is designed with one absolute priority: strict respect for your privacy. Your decision-weather calculations, time windows, synergy readings and compass are performed directly on your iPhone. The app does not sell any data and displays no advertising.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Full birth date, birth time and form of address: stored only locally on your device (and shared locally with your iOS widgets) so the app can display your personal alignment and orientations.
  • Profiles of relatives and associates (Synergy mode): first names and birth dates you add remain exclusively on your device.
  • Journal notes: the free-text notes you write stay stored locally on your iPhone.

You can erase your local data from within the app or by uninstalling QiMétéo.

3. Apple Calendar

With your explicit permission, QiMétéo reads the titles and times of your upcoming events in order to compare them with the day’s score and Golden Windows, and lets you add a favourable window to your calendar in one tap. QiMétéo does not modify your other events and never sends the contents of your calendar to a server.

4. Compass & Heading

When you open the energy compass, the app requests access “while using the app” solely to read your iPhone’s magnetic heading and show the live orientation. No GPS coordinates are recorded or transmitted. If you decline, a manual slider remains available.

5. Notifications

The morning briefing and window reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notification is sent from an external server.

6. Purchases & Subscriptions (Apple & RevenueCat)

QiMétéo Premium subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store and verified through RevenueCat (to manage the “premium” entitlement and restore purchases, using a pseudonymous identifier and the Apple AdServices attribution token where applicable). QiMétéo never has access to your payment details.

7. Pseudonymous Usage Measurement (EU hosting, no IP address)

Distributed versions send pseudonymous usage events to our analytics tool Mixpanel, hosted in the European Union, with IP-address collection disabled. No cross-app advertising identifier (IDFA) is used.

These anonymised statistics help improve the app, using a random identifier unique to the installation (device model, language, subscription status, screens viewed, year of birth, main onboarding goal, and the journal’s mood rating).

8. Retention, Deletion & Contact

For any question, or to request deletion of the analytics data associated with your device identifier, write to: xlarrodeapp+qimeteo@gmail.com
