Support

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Short answers to the most frequent questions. QiMétéo is available for iPhone (iOS 17+). Offers are displayed in the app.

Contact

For any question: xlarrodeapp+qimeteo@gmail.com

Manage a subscription

QiMétéo Premium subscriptions are managed by the Apple App Store. To view, change or cancel them: iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Cancellation is done in your Apple ID account, at least 24 hours before renewal. Apple Family Sharing is included for up to six members.

Restore a purchase

Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and managed by the App Store. To recover a subscription you already bought, sign in with the same Apple ID on your iPhone (or use “Restore purchases” in the Profile tab): the App Store restores the purchases linked to that account. QiMétéo does not have access to your payment details.

Widgets

The daily edition includes Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets on iOS. They rely on calculations performed on your device (birth date and time, Gua number) and do not send any personal data to an advertising server.

Free trial

QiMétéo Premium, monthly and annual, includes a 7-day free trial, with Family Sharing included. The Premium subscription (monthly or annual) renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date in your Apple ID account.
