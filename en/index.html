II · The Tong Shu Almanac

Three skies to read any day at a glance — without confusing Success and Full days.*

In classical Tong Shu, every day is governed by one of the 12 Officers (Jian Chu). QiMétéo crosses that Officer against your 4 Birth Pillars, strictly distinguishes Full/Accumulation from Success, and filters out Kong Wang void hours.*

Bright Sun & Clearing
成 · 開 · 定

Supportive Day — Success, Open & Stable

Success (成), Open (開), Stable (定), or Establish (建) days free of clashes against your natal chart. Favorable for contracts, launches, negotiations, and milestone commitments.*

Calm & Nuanced Sky
滿 · 收 · 除

Targeted Day — Nuance on Full / Accumulation (滿)

The Full Officer (滿) differs from Success (成) or Harvest (收): it symbolically amplifies whatever is started. Lean on good Qi Men hourly windows* (and for any medical care, always follow your doctor’s schedule exclusively**).

Rain, Storm & Kong Wang
破 · 閉 · 空亡

Restraint — Destruction, Natal Clash & Void Hours

Destruction (破), Close (閉), or direct Clash days against your Day Pillar: favorable for reviewing, consolidating, and postponing what can wait. Kong Wang (空亡) void hours are automatically excluded.*

VII · Frequently asked questions

Everything to know about the method, compass, and free trial.

Why enter both my birth date and birth hour? +

Your birth-year animal is only one-eighth of your natal chart. QiMétéo computes all 4 Solar Pillars (Li Chun Year, JieQi Month, Day, and Hour): your Birth Day Element (Day Master) anchors your daily personal alignment, while your Birth Hour refines your 3 daily 2-hour Qi Men Dun Jia windows.

How is QiMétéo different from Co-Star, The Pattern, or a Chinese lunar calendar? +

Western astrology apps focus on psychological reflection without tactical 2-hour execution windows or directional compasses. Standard Tong Shu / lunar apps give the exact same rating to everyone, missing personal clashes against your Day Pillar. QiMétéo crosses your 4 natal Pillars with Tong Shu, Qi Men Dun Jia, and Ming Gua Feng Shui in 3 seconds.*

How do the two Live Compass modes ('2h Slots Qi Men' vs. 'Lifetime At Home Gua') differ? +

'2h Slots (Qi Men)' is temporal and updates every two hours: it shows which direction to turn your back toward during a key call, pitch, or signing, paired with a 3-minute focus ritual.* 'Lifetime • At Home (Gua)' is permanent: based on your solar birth year, it gives your 4 lifetime ±7° azimuths for positioning your desk and bed headboard.*

What do Full (Accumulation) days, Kong Wang void hours, and Health labels mean? +

In classical Tong Shu, a Full / Accumulation day (滿) symbolically multiplies what is begun and is kept distinct from Success (成); likewise, Kong Wang (空亡, Celestial Void) hours are automatically filtered out of recommended windows.* Important: every indicator in QiMétéo is a cultural, symbolic reading with zero medical value — always follow your physician’s advice and schedule exclusively.**

How does Love, Family & Business Associate Synergy work? +

Enter a partner’s, relative’s, or co-founder’s birth date (and hour if known). QiMétéo translates your dynamic into clear, everyday English with zero esoteric jargon: core motivation, stress response, autonomy needs, friction points to avoid, and your highest-scoring shared dates over 30 days.*
