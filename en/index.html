II · The Tong Shu Almanac

Three readable skies at a glance — never confusing Success with Accumulation.*

Each day is governed by one of the 12 Day Officers (Jian Chu). QiMétéo crosses that Officer with your 4 Natal Pillars, distinguishes Success (成) from Full/Accumulation (滿), and filters out Kong Wang hours.*

Bright Sun & Clearing
成 · 開 · 定

Supportive Day — Success, Open & Stable

Days of Success (成), Open (開), or Stable (定) free of personal pillar clashes: ideal to sign contracts, launch projects, or negotiate.*

Calm & Nuanced Sky
滿 · 收 · 除

Targeted Day — Full / Accumulation (滿) Nuance

The Full Officer (滿) symbolically amplifies whatever you initiate: lean on your top 2h Qi Men windows* (for any medical care, always follow your doctor’s schedule exclusively**).

Rain, Storm & Kong Wang
破 · 閉 · 空亡

Hold Back — Destruction, Personal Clash, or Void

Days of Destruction (破), Closed (閉), or direct clash with your Birth Day Pillar: consolidate, review, and postpone non-urgent commitments.*

VII · Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about the method, dual compass, and 7-day free trial.

Why enter both my birth date and my birth hour? +

Because your birth-year animal represents only one-eighth of your natal chart. QiMétéo computes all 4 Solar Pillars (Solar Year after Li Chun, Solar Month JieQi, Birth Day, and Birth Hour): your Birth Day Element is your personal daily anchor, while your Birth Hour calibrates your 3 daily 2-hour Qi Men Dun Jia windows.

How is QiMétéo different from Co-Star or a generic lunar calendar? +

Western apps like Co-Star or CHANI focus on psychological planetary transits without 2-hour tactical windows or spatial orientation. Public lunar almanacs show the exact same 'lucky day' to everyone without detecting a direct clash against your Birth Day Pillar. QiMétéo synthesizes your 4 Natal Pillars, Tong Shu, Qi Men Dun Jia, and Ming Gua in 3 seconds.*

What is the difference between the two Compass modes: 2h Windows (Qi Men) vs Lifetime Home (Gua)? +

The '2h Windows (Qi Men)' mode updates every two hours and shows which direction to turn your back toward during an important call, negotiation, or signing, complete with a 3-minute focus ritual.* The 'Lifetime Home (Gua)' mode is permanent: computed from your solar birth year, it reveals your 4 lifetime Ming Gua directions for positioning your desk and bed headboard.*

What do the Accumulation (滿) nuance, Kong Wang hours, and Health notes mean? +

In classical Tong Shu, a Full/Accumulation (滿) day symbolically amplifies what is initiated and is carefully distinguished from Success (成); likewise, Kong Wang (空亡, Celestial Void) hours are automatically filtered out.* Important: every indicator in QiMétéo is a cultural, symbolic reading with zero medical value — never delay, advance, or alter any medical appointment, test, or treatment based on this app; follow your physician exclusively.**

How does Love, Family & Business Associate Synergy work? +

Enter the birth date (and birth hour if known) of a partner, relative, or co-founder — saved locally on your device. QiMétéo translates your real interpersonal dynamics in plain English: decision style, autonomy needs, response under stress, friction points to avoid, and your best shared dates over 30 days.*
